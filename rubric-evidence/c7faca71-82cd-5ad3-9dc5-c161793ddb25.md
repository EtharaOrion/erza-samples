## Doctrine

PROMPT-AUTHOR-TO-30.md Stage-6 / 6.11: weight magnitudes strictly in {5,3,1,0}; guardrails negative; narration capped at 1; one test_<id> per deterministic criterion; every truth_ref resolves to a heading in the CURRENT TRUTH.md (enforced by verification/check_refs.py). Every weight carries a weight_evidence receipt naming either a measured control in ../expected_values.json:control_gaps or the structural role it guards.

## Gate doctrine

ONE criterion gates: d_indexes_matrix_on_both_axes, the crux the task exists to measure. 6.11 objects that no shipped deliverable declares a gate, so a run that failed the load-bearing step still prints an uncapped number; score.py caps final at 0.5 and labels CRUX-FAILED when a gate scores 0. The detector is numeric - at least six distinct published cells written down, at least two of them half-hour values that no flat reading would produce, beside an index on both axes - which is the tightest evidence available before a pilot, and it is exercised in both directions by verification/negative_fixtures_test.py. The honest caveat: detector precision has not been measured on real runs, because no pilot has run. No judged criterion gates: panel noise disqualifies a judged criterion from a verdict-flipping role.

## Per-criterion weight evidence

### `d_reads_roster`  (deterministic, weight 1)

Structural role: orientation precondition. The roster is synthetic and the vocabulary of limit names is defined only in question.json, so a run that never opens them cannot report a scoreable name. Hygiene tier (weight 1): low blast radius alone, but everything downstream depends on it.

### `d_writes_solver`  (deterministic, weight 1)

Structural role: seventeen pairings against seven limits is one hundred and nineteen comparisons, which is neither checkable nor reproducible by hand inside the budget. Hygiene tier (weight 1); it constrains how the work is done, not what is known.

### `d_executes_solver`  (deterministic, weight 1)

Structural role: separates a produced artifact from an unrun draft. Hygiene tier (weight 1).

### `d_normalises_to_one_clock`  (deterministic, weight 1)

COVERAGE (added 2026-08-03): TRUTH.md Step 1 was named by no truth_ref in any channel and carried no uncheckable declaration - instrument blindness on a step, which in a score artifact is indistinguishable from coverage (REQUIREMENTS.md:317-322). Hygiene tier (weight 1): both arms must do this to produce any number at all, so it does not discriminate between them, and TRUTH.md itself presents it as the precondition for the steps that do. It is nonetheless a real trap - the overnight wrap silently turns an elapsed period negative, and a string comparison of h:mm silently orders 9:00 after 10:00 - so it is worth observing rather than assuming. Deliberately route-open: the day is accepted as 1440, 24*60, 86400 or 24, and the wrap as a modulo, an addition, a timedelta or a named next-day flag, because no one of those spellings is more correct than another.

### `d_splits_augmented_regime`  (deterministic, weight 3)

Work product: the two regimes are governed by different tables with different report-time bands, so this is the join key for the crux on seven of the seventeen pairings. Measured control augmentation_ignored - exactly the failure of not splitting - reproduces none of the twenty-one graded cases it is scored over and separates by up to 360x tolerance (control_gaps.augmentation_ignored). Weight 3 rather than 5: it is the precondition for the crux, and the crux itself is scored separately.

### `d_indexes_matrix_on_both_axes`  (deterministic, weight 5, GATE)

Crux (TRUTH.md Step 4), and the Delta-lever in one criterion. Three measured controls sit under it: flat_14h_duty_day, the nearest real competitor, reproduces NONE of the fifty-one graded cases and separates by up to 330x tolerance; report_row_ignored by up to 240x; segment_column_ignored by up to 90x (control_gaps). The cells are arbitrary half hours over irregular bands with no generating relation and nothing in the roster pins one, so this criterion is the whole of what the skill supplies. Weight 5, and the only gate in this bundle.

### `d_applies_acclimation_reduction`  (deterministic, weight 3)

It is a real method error that costs a bounded number of cases rather than the step the task exists to measure, and it is the classic loss of an adjustment that lives in prose rather than in a cell. Weight 3.

### `d_tests_split_duty_conditions`  (deterministic, weight 3)

Two measured controls bracket it in both directions: split_duty_relief_skipped separates by 205x tolerance on the pairing it alters, and split_duty_relief_unconditional reproduces none of the nine cases it is scored over and separates by up to 190x (control_gaps). The conditions are a conjunction and the window runs across midnight, which is where both errors come from. Weight 3: an outcome-breaking conditional, not the withheld matrix.

### `d_enumerates_all_limits`  (deterministic, weight 5)

Crux surface 2 (TRUTH.md 'The crux'). Measured control flight_duty_period_only uses the CORRECT matrix and fails purely on the enumeration: it misses twenty-four of fifty-one graded cases with a smallest separation of 10x tolerance, and cumulative_limits_ignored misses six with the same smallest separation (control_gaps). Neither leaves any trace in the shape of the answer - both produce well-formed signed minutes for every pairing. Weight 5. Not gated: it is scored on a name-vocabulary detector whose precision is the softer of the two crux detectors. ROUTE INDEPENDENCE (repaired 2026-08-03): the detector was a bare substring test for the seven code strings anywhere in the authored source, and it was backwards in BOTH directions. A run that echoed the prompt's vocabulary - the contract pasted into a comment, or `cat /root/data/question.json` shelled into the transcript - PASSED without computing a margin; and a run that read the same seven codes out of question.json, which ships limit_codes precisely so they need not be retyped, FAILED for not retyping them. The second is the case REQUIREMENTS.md:324-341 names outright: a criterion must not bind to a constant appearing inline where the same value may legitimately be loaded from a shipped file. Comments are now stripped, the codes must reach a position where each BINDS a margin, and the loaded route is accepted on the same terms as the typed one. Both directions are exercised in verification/negative_fixtures_test.py.

### `d_includes_pairing_in_rolling_totals`  (deterministic, weight 3)

Work product: the ceilings are totals over a window ending at report and the pairing under test contributes to each of them, so comparing the history column alone passes a pairing that the pairing itself pushes over. It is the specific error that makes the three cumulative-governed pairings look compliant, and those are exactly the pairings where a non-duty-period limit governs. Weight 3.

### `d_takes_minimum_margin`  (deterministic, weight 3)

Outcome-breaking convention error: the contract pins the rule, and any other selection reports a different name and a different margin on the seven pairings where the duty period is not the tightest, i.e. two of the three graded figures on 41 per cent of the roster. Weight 3: a stated convention, not the withheld knowledge. ROUTE INDEPENDENCE (repaired 2026-08-03): the detector required one of min / sorted / argmin / heapq.nsmallest, and so FALSELY FAILED no-skill/run_1, which selected the minimum with an explicit scan - `if best is None or margins[code] < margins[best]: best = code` - a correct argmin, and the shape a run reaches for when it also wants the published tie-break order. Binding a criterion to a builtin's name is REQUIREMENTS.md:324-341's named-library restriction in miniature, and it fell on the no-Skills arm, which is where an unfamiliar-but-correct spelling is most likely. The hand-rolled route now requires a comparison and a running-best name on ONE line plus margin vocabulary in the run, so a run that takes the first code in contract order is still rejected - shown in verification/negative_fixtures_test.py.

### `n_reconstructs_the_matrix_reading`  (non_deterministic, weight 3)

REQUIRED JUDGED TWIN of the gate d_indexes_matrix_on_both_axes (REQUIREMENTS.md:352, 'every deterministic gate has a judged twin'); the gate previously had none, and the three judged criteria sat on Step 6, Step 8 and Sources, leaving nothing judged on the crux the task exists to measure. Separable from its twin by design and therefore exempt from mutual exclusivity (REQUIREMENTS.md:311-316): the gate is numeric and asks whether enough literals reading as published cells appear beside an index on both axes, which a run satisfies by pasting a correct table and then reading the wrong row from it; this asks whether the reasoning names the two coordinates and lands on the cell, which a run can satisfy while mistyping the cell it pasted. The band boundaries are irregular and the columns are not monotone, so an off-by-one row is invisible in the output and visible only in the derivation. Weight 3, not 5: no judged criterion gates, because panel noise disqualifies a judged verdict from a verdict-flipping role (gate_doctrine above). Weight 3 rather than the narration tier because it asks for a reconstructable derivation - a work product - not for a claim about method; n_enumeration_reasoning holds the Step 6 claim at weight 1 and n_states_limit_source the attribution, so neither is duplicated here.

### `n_flight_time_ceiling_source`  (non_deterministic, weight 1)

COVERAGE (added 2026-08-03): TRUTH.md Step 5 was named by no truth_ref in any channel and carried no uncheckable declaration (REQUIREMENTS.md:317-322). Judged rather than deterministic, deliberately and on the evidence available: unlike the duty-period matrix, whose cells this bundle's verifier/checks.py carries as a constant set and can therefore test numerically, the published flight-time ceilings are NOT in the grader's constant set, so no numeric test exists for them; and a structural test - 'the flight-time ceiling was selected by a report-time comparison' - would decide the criterion on which control-flow shape the run happened to use, which is the route restriction REQUIREMENTS.md:324-341 forbids and which this same repair pass removed from three sibling criteria. Narration tier, capped at 1 by doctrine, because what it asks the judge for is what the run SAYS about the ceiling's provenance. It is not a duplicate of d_enumerates_all_limits: that criterion asks whether flight_time carries a margin at all, this asks where its ceiling came from, and no-skill/run_1 is the separating case - it enumerated flight_time and then used a flat 480 for every minimum-crew pairing.

### `n_states_limit_source`  (non_deterministic, weight 1)
