### `note`

Every criterion is a question about the agent's process, derived from TRUTH.md. `channel` decides who answers it: `outcome` -> a pytest function of the same id in verifier/test_trajectory.py that re-derives the run's final answer from its own trajectory by probe re-execution; `deterministic` -> a pytest function of the same id in the same file, reading the trajectory's mechanics; `non_deterministic` -> the LLM judge in judge/judge.py. `is_positive: false` marks a guardrail - satisfied means the bad behaviour actually occurred, and it subtracts. TRUTH.md is an answer-free golden trajectory: it states what to do at each step and never what the step evaluates to, so no PROCESS criterion may be graded by comparing against a ground-truth value. The `outcome` channel is the deliberate exception the pipeline's Stage 4 defines: it grades the answer itself against the task's own frozen truth in verifier/expected_values.json, never against TRUTH.md. truth_ref values name headings of the CURRENT TRUTH.md (Step N / Step Na / closing section titles) and are validated by verification/check_refs.py - run it whenever TRUTH.md is restructured.

### `weights_doctrine`

Weights follow the Stage 6 doctrine of VERIFIER_PIPELINE.md: 5 = crux (is_gate: true only for deterministic or outcome, control-measured, precision~1 criteria; a failed deterministic gate caps final at 0.5 and prints CRUX-FAILED, a failed outcome gate caps identically and prints OUTCOME-FAILED, and both can fire together), 3 = outcome-breaking or behavioural-with-work-product, 1 = hygiene/narration (narration never exceeds 1), 0 = report-only (unstable). Every weight carries its receipt in weight_evidence. Channels blend by weight mass. Sources of evidence: build/build_report.json controls, the 32-run discrimination matrix (in-sample), and TRUTH.md structural roles.


### `mece`

REQ-9: the criterion set is MECE across the outcome and process instruments jointly. MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE - no two criteria are decided by the same evidence; for every pair there is a trajectory that fails exactly one, argued in each criterion's weight_evidence. The deliberate exception is a d_ criterion and its judged twin on the same subject: they rest on different evidence (what the code did vs. what the reasoning earned) and are separable in both directions. COLLECTIVELY EXHAUSTIVE - see the `coverage` block. THREE CHANNELS - `outcome` (o_*: pytest that re-derives the final answer by probe re-execution of the run's own solver), `deterministic` (d_*: mechanics over the trajectory), `non_deterministic` (n_*: the judged rubric) - blended by weight mass with TWO gates capping identically at 0.5: CRUX-FAILED and OUTCOME-FAILED.

### R1

**`weight_evidence`**

Structural role: the outcome channel's shape check. Weight 3 (contract conformance, outcome-breaking): a correct calculation serialised outside the declared contract scores zero on the reward instrument. Route-independent - it grades the answer's shape, never how the answer was reached, and it is satisfied by a solver in any language or by a hand-written answer file. Not a nested duplicate of R2: the separating trajectory is the common one that emits a well-formed answer with a wrong value, which fails the tolerance criterion alone.


### R2

**`weight_evidence`**

GATE, weight 5: this is the outcome itself, and REQ-9 requires the wrong-answer gate (OUTCOME-FAILED) to cap final at 0.5 exactly as CRUX-FAILED does. The reference and the tolerance are the task's own frozen, control-measured values in verifier/expected_values.json - not a value invented for the rubric. Route-independent by construction: it reads only the answer, so every correct route satisfies it. Outcome mass is deliberately modest (8 of the rubric's total weight mass) - the channel's authority is the gate, not the mass.
